# Consent

- **What we ask:** a 20-minute timed task (deciding whether you would merge agent-produced work, with and without our tool), optionally installing the tool for one week, and optionally sharing up to five recent coding-agent session logs.

- **Your rights:** you can stop at any time and ask for your data to be deleted; participation has no effect on anything else.
- **Contact:** the team members listed in the repository README.

Reply "I consent" to take part. Thank you.
